## Recommended range

**× 12–13, i.e. ~1,890–2,050 kcal/day.** Gives 200–370 g/week — slow enough to be mostly fat, and slow enough not to interfere with the elbow rehab.

**Protein: 115–160 g/day** (1.6–2.2 g/kg). This is what decides whether the loss comes off as fat or as muscle. Also supports tendon repair.

## Caveats

- Maintenance is an estimate with roughly ±15% error — the real figure could be anywhere from ~1,950 to ~2,700. Two to three weeks of weekly-average weigh-ins beats any formula.
- Predicted losses are optimistic at the steep end. Maintenance drifts down as weight comes off, and appetite plus unconscious movement both push back. × 10 tends to deliver a few good weeks, then a stall and flat training sessions.
- Nothing above × 13 is worth chasing at this bodyweight. The muscle-preservation margin is thinner at 71.6 kg than it would be at 90 kg, and the Tyler protocol repairs better with fuel behind it.
- First 1–2 weeks include water and glycogen changes, which are calorically "cheap" — don't back-calculate your deficit from a short stretch.

## Targets

- **Goal weight:** ~66 kg (top of the ethnicity-adjusted healthy BMI band). About 5.5 kg, so 3–4 months at this pace.
- **Floor:** don't plan below ~63 kg while strength training.
- **Waist:** under 33.5 in / 85 cm. Measure at the midpoint between lowest rib and top of hip bone, against skin, at the end of a normal breath out. Responds faster and more meaningfully than the scale.
