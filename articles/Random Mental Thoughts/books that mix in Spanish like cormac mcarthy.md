- **The Brief Wondrous Life of Oscar Wao** by Junot Díaz — heavy Spanglish code-switching, very conversational and slangy rather than McCarthy's more formal/literary Spanish.
- **Drown**, also Díaz — short stories, same style, less commitment if you want to sample it first.
- **How the García Girls Lost Their Accents** by Julia Alvarez — Dominican-American family story, Spanish threaded through more gently than McCarthy or Díaz.
- **Bless Me, Ultima** by Rudolfo Anaya — New Mexico setting, similar rural/dusty atmosphere to McCarthy actually, with Spanish dialogue used the same unexplained way.
- **Caramelo** by Sandra Cisneros — Mexican-American, playful with language, lots of code-switching.
