## Cooking instructions

Instructions for 2 \[for 3\] \[for 4\] portion recipe

1. ![Step 1](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-1-1598262414904-x400.jpg)
	1.
	Add the **lemongrass**, **basmati rice** and 300ml \[600ml\] **cold water** to a pot with a lid and bring to the boil over a high heat
	Once boiling, reduce the heat to very low and cook, covered, for 10-15 min or until all the water has absorbed and the rice is cooked
	Once cooked, remove from the heat and keep covered until serving
2. ![Step 2](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-2-1598262422502-x400.jpg)
	2.
	Meanwhile, boil half a kettle
	Peel and finely chop the **shallot\[s\]**
	Chop the **green beans** into bite-sized pieces
3. ![Step 3](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-3-1602153376533-x400.jpg)
	3.
	Peel the **ginger** and **garlic**
	Slice the **red chilli\[es\]** in half and scrape the seeds out with a spoon
	Chop the **coriander** roughly, reserving some leaves for garnish
	Add the **ginger**, **garlic**, **half** ofthe **red chilli** (save the rest for later) and **chopped coriander** to a **food processor** with 2 tbsp \[4 tbsp\] **water** and blitz until smooth – this is your **Thai green curry paste**
4. ![Step 4](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/Step-4-1598262481180-x400.jpg)
	4.
	Heat a large, wide-based pan (preferably non-stick) with a drizzle of **vegetable oil** over a high heat
	Once hot, add the **shallot** with a pinch of **salt** and cook for 1 min
	Once the shallot has softened, add the **beef strips** and cook for 2-3 min or until starting to brown
5. ![Step 5](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-5-1598262494503-x400.jpg)
	5.
	Once browned, add the **Thai green curry paste** to the pan with the beef and cook for 2 min or until fragrant
	Meanwhile, dissolve the **coconut cream** in 150ml \[300ml\] **boiled water**, add the **fish sauce** with the **lime leaves**, the **juice** of **1/2** \[1\] **lime** and 1 tsp \[2 tsp\] **sugar** – this is your **fragrant stock**
6. ![Step 6](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-6-1598262517648-x400.jpg)
	6.
	Add the **chopped green beans** and **fragrant stock** to the pan and cook for 4 min or until the beef is cooked through and the sauce has thickened
	Tip: Add an extra splash of water if the sauce thickens too quickly!
7. ![Step 7](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-7-1598262523097-x400.jpg)
	7.
	While the beef is cooking, cut the **remaining** **lime** into wedges
	Slice the **remaining** **chilli** finely
	Serve the **Thai green beef curry** over the **cooked lemongrass rice**
	Tip: For fancy presentation, press the rice into small bowls and turn out
	Garnish with the **chopped chilli** (can't handle the heat? Go easy!), **coriander leaves** and a **lime wedge**
	Enjoy!
