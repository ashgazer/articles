## Cooking instructions

Instructions for 2 \[for 3\] \[for 4\] portion recipe

1. ![Step 1](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-1-1705677858497-x400.jpg)
	1.
	**Before you begin...**
	Get all your ingredients and equipment ready
	Skim through these steps, then wash your fruit and veg
	Note: Equipment and pan sizes vary. Make sure you cook food thoroughly (even if it takes you a little longer than 10 min)
2. ![Step 2](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-2-1705677862719-x400.jpg)
	2.
	**Now, let's get started!**
	Boil a kettle
	Heat a large, wide-based pan (preferably non-stick) with a drizzle of **vegetable oil** over a high heat
	Once hot, add your **mince** and cook for 3-4 min or until beginning to brown, breaking it up into very small pieces with a wooden spoon as you go
	(Doubled your protein? Use 2 pans!)
3. ![Step 3](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-3-1705677866714-x400.jpg)
	3.
	While the mince is cooking, chop your **creamed coconut** roughly (if required!)
	Wash, then cut your **lettuce** into wedges
	Slice your **red chilli\[es\]** finely
4. ![Step 4](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-4-1705677870635-x400.jpg)
	4.
	Once the mince is beginning to brown, add your **ground cinnamon** (not a fan of cinnamon? Only add a little!) with your **ground turmeric** and **half** the **sliced chilli** (can't handle the heat? Go easy!) and cook for 30 secs or until fragrant
	Once fragrant, add the **chopped creamed coconut** with your **tamarind paste**, **ginger & garlic paste** and **half** your **peanut butter** (you'll use the rest later!)
5. ![Step 5](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-5-1705677874517-x400.jpg)
	5.
	Add **half** your **sriracha** (save the rest for later!) with your **soy sauce**, **beef stock mix** and 150ml \[195ml\] \[250ml\] **boiled water**
	Cook for a further 4-5 min or until the mince is cooked through and the sauce has thickened (no pink meat!) – this is your **rendang-inspired mince curry**
6. ![Step 6](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-6-1705677896821-x400.jpg)
	6.
	While the curry is cooking, combine the **remaining** **peanut butter** with your **white wine vinegar** and **remaining sriracha**
	Add a pinch of **salt** and 1 tsp \[1 1/2 tsp\] \[2 tsp\] **sugar** with a little **olive oil** and mix it all together – this is your **satay dressing**
7. ![Step 7](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-7-1705677901060-x400.jpg)
	7.
	Squeeze your pouch\[es\] of **cooked long grain rice** to separate the grains
	Tear the top corner of the pouch\[es\] (just a little!) and **microwave** for 2 min or until piping hot
	Tip: If you're cooking two or more pouches, pop them in together but increase the microwave cook time accordingly
	Serve the **rendang-inspired mince curry** over the **rice** and garnish with the **remaining sliced chilli** (not a fan of spice? Just add a little!)
	Serve the **lettuce wedges** to the side and drizzle over the **satay dressing** – this is your **satay salad**
	Enjoy!
