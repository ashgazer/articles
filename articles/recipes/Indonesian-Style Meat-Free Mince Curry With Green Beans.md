## Cooking instructions

Instructions for 2 \[for 3\] \[for 4\] portion recipe

1. ![Step 1](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/Step-1-1633089191745-x400.jpg)
	1.
	Add your **basmati rice** and 300ml \[390ml\] \[600ml\] **cold water** to a pot with a lid and bring to the boil over a high heat
	Once boiling, reduce the heat to very low and cook, covered, for 10-12 min or until all the water has absorbed and the rice is cooked
	Once cooked, remove from the heat and keep covered until serving
2. ![Step 2](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/Step-2-1633089195390-x400.jpg)
	2.
	While the rice is cooking, boil a kettle
	Peel and finely slice your **brown onion\[s\]**
	Heat a large, wide-based pan (preferably non-stick) with a drizzle of **vegetable oil** over a medium-high heat
	Once hot, add the **sliced onion** and cook for 4-5 min or until beginning to soften
3. ![Step 3](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/Step-3-1633089198590-x400.jpg)
	3.
	Meanwhile, peel (scrape the skin off with a teaspoon) and finely chop (or grate) your **ginger**
	Peel and finely chop (or grate) your **garlic**
	Break your **cardamom pods** open with the side of a knife, discarding the outer green pods
4. ![Step 4](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/Step-4-1633089202358-x400.jpg)
	4.
	Once the onion has started to soften, add your **meat-free mince** (remove the paper if required!) to the pan and cook for 6-7 min or until starting to brown, breaking it up with a wooden spoon as you go
5. ![Step 5](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/Step-5-1633089205379-x400.jpg)
	5.
	Chop your **creamed coconut** roughly (if required!)
	Dissolve the **chopped creamed coconut**, your **vegetable stock mix**, **tamarind paste** and a generous pinch of **sugar** in 400ml \[550ml\] \[700ml\] **boiled water** – this is your **coconut stock**
	Trim then cut your **green beans** in half
6. ![Step 6](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/Step-6-1633089208083-x400.jpg)
	6.
	Once the meat-free mince has browned, add the **chopped garlic**, **chopped** **ginger** and **cardamom seeds** and cook for 1 min or until fragrant
	Add your **ground turmeric**, **ground coriander** and **ground cumin** with a very generous grind of **black pepper** and cook for 1 min further
7. ![Step 7](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/Step-7-1633089211213-x400.jpg)
	7.
	Add the **halved green beans** to the pan with the **coconut stock** and bring to the boil over a high heat
	Cook for 5-6 min or until the sauce has reduced to a curry-like consistency – this is your **Indonesian-style meat-free mince & green bean curry**
	Serve the **Indonesian-style meat-free mince & green bean curry** with the **basmati rice** to the side
	Garnish with your **crispy onions**
	Enjoy!
