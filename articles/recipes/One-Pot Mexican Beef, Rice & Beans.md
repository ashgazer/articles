## Cooking instructions

Instructions for 2 \[for 3\] \[for 4\] portion recipe

1. ![Step 1](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-1-1641911061617-x400.jpg)
	1.
	Deseed your **peppers** (scrape the seeds and pith out with a teaspoon) and chop into bite-sized pieces
	Peel and slice your **brown onion\[s\]**
2. ![Step 2](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-2-1641911068910-x400.jpg)
	2.
	Heat a large, wide-based pan (preferably non-stick with a matching lid) with a drizzle of **vegetable oil** over a medium-high heat
	Once hot, add the **sliced onion** and **chopped peppers** with a generous pinch of **salt**
	Cook for 2 min or until starting to soften
3. ![Step 3](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-3-1641911076919-x400.jpg)
	3.
	Add your **ground cumin**, **ground coriander**, **ground** **smoked paprika** and **half** your **chilli flakes** (can't handle the heat? Go easy!) and cook for 1-2 min or until fragrant
4. ![Step 4](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-4-1641911086116-x400.jpg)
	4.
	Once fragrant, increase the heat to high and add your **mince** to the pan and cook for 2 min, breaking it up with a wooden spoon as you go
	Mix in the **beef stock mix** and **tomato paste** with a pinchof **salt** and cook for a further 2 min or until the mincehas browned all over
5. ![Step 5](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-5-1641911094867-x400.jpg)
	5.
	Add your **white long grain rice**, **chipotle paste** (not a fan of spice? Just add a little!), a pinch of **salt** and **pepper** and 175ml \[230ml\] \[350ml\] **cold water** to the pan and bring to the boil over a high heat
	Once boiling, reduce the heat to low and cook, covered, for 12-15 min or until all the water is absorbed and the rice and meat is cooked (no pink meat!)
	Tip: Add a splash more water if it's looking a little dry!
6. ![Step 6](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-6-1641911104691-x400.jpg)
	6.
	Meanwhile, drain and rinse your **black beans**
7. ![Step 7](https://production-media.gousto.co.uk/cms/recipe-step-image/step-7-1641911112460-x400.jpg)
	7.
	Once the rice is cooked, stir through the **drained** **black beans** and cook for a further 2 min or until the beans are warmed through – this is your **Mexican-style mince**, **rice & beans**
	Serve the **one pot** **Mexican-style mince**, **rice & beans** topped with a dollop of your **natural yoghurt** and the **remaining chilli flakes** (not a fan of spice? Just add a little!)
	Enjoy!
