interesting at the start this book did not grab me like [[The Quiet American - Graham Greene| the quiet American]]. However, this book has caught my attention, again Greene is so good at building characters in such a short space of time, you get to know their motivations and really can understand why they work the way they do. I love the fact nothing is clear cut. The lieutenant a hard man with deadly ambitions, but Greene still manages to portray him as a human being, who has a good side to him. He gives the priest 5 pesos when he realises the man has no chance of work at his age and has no money.
