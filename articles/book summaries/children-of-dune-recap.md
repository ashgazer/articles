# Children of Dune — Recap to the Laza Tiger Attack

*Frank Herbert · Gollancz edition · rebuilt up to the moment Leto asks Ghanima for her blade to make sure of the kill (around p.192). **Nothing beyond that scene is revealed.***

---

## Where you are

It's roughly nine years since Paul walked blind into the desert to die. His twins, Leto and Ghanima, are only nine, but they're *pre-born*: conscious in the womb, carrying the full flood of ancestral memories — which is exactly the condition the Bene Gesserit dread, because it's what broke Alia.

Alia is the slow-motion catastrophe running underneath everything. As Regent she's been holding the Empire together, but she's losing the internal war — the ego-memory of Baron Harkonnen, her maternal grandfather, has surfaced inside her and is steadily taking her over. That's the "Abomination" the order warned about. Her rule and the Muad'Dib priesthood have curdled into something rigid and paranoid.

That's largely why Jessica has come back to Arrakis: sent to assess the twins, to judge whether they've fallen the way Alia has. Her return sharpens every tension in Arrakeen. Meanwhile two other pressures build — the greening of Arrakis is quietly threatening the worms and therefore the spice, and a blind Preacher has appeared in the streets, thundering against what Alia's made of Paul's religion. His identity is one of the book's big live questions.

Then the tigers. House Corrino — deposed, exiled on Salusa Secundus — wants the throne back. Wensicia, Shaddam's daughter, is the schemer driving it, and her son Farad'n is the piece she's positioning. It's Wensicia who engineered this: two Laza tigers, specially conditioned to hunt and kill the twins. So the animals coming at Leto and Ghanima are an assassination, not a wilderness accident. And the twins aren't ambushed — through Leto's visions they knew this was coming and walked out to meet it deliberately, armed and rehearsed. That's why the scene reads so cold.

---

## The setup, thread by thread

### The world: nine years on
Arrakis is being deliberately transformed — plantations, open water, the desert receding under the ecological programme Liet-Kynes once dreamed of. It sounds like paradise, but it's an existential threat: the shrinking desert endangers the sandworms, and no worms means no spice. Fremen culture is eroding alongside the dunes, and old Fremen resent what the planet is becoming. This slow crisis sits under all the political manoeuvring.

### The twins: what they are, and what they've decided
The book opens with Stilgar standing over the sleeping twins, deeply uneasy — even turning over the thought of killing them, because he senses how dangerous they are. That establishes the Abomination anxiety from page one.

Leto and Ghanima live a strange double life: nine-year-old bodies, but minds crowded with the consciousness of countless ancestors. They speak to each other as equals with millennia of perspective. They've already concluded that Alia is lost, that they themselves are marked for death — by the Sisterhood, by Alia's regime, by the Corrinos — and they've begun working toward *Secher Nbiw*, the **Golden Path**: a vision (mostly Leto's) of the one route that avoids Abomination and saves humanity from a much longer-term extinction. Leto has also quietly set an arrangement with Stilgar to protect Ghanima if anything happens to him.

### Alia: the enemy inside
Alia rules as Regent, but she's being hollowed out from within. Among the ancestral egos she carries, the **Baron Vladimir Harkonnen** — her own grandfather — has risen and is seizing the controls. This is precisely the Abomination the Bene Gesserit feared in a pre-born child. Her regime and the Muad'Dib priesthood (Javid and the rest) have hardened into something corrupt and paranoid. She is married to **Duncan Idaho**, a Tleilaxu ghola of the original Duncan, who grows more and more disturbed as he watches what she's turning into.

### Jessica returns
Lady Jessica arrives back on Arrakis from Caladan, with **Gurney Halleck** at her side, ostensibly on Bene Gesserit business: to assess the twins. Her return immediately alarms Alia, who suspects a plot to take the children. Jessica reads Alia almost at once — she sees the possession — but when she tests Leto and Ghanima she finds them, for now, still clear of Abomination. The friction between Jessica and Alia tightens with every scene.

### The Preacher
A blind Preacher has come out of the desert and walks the streets of Arrakeen, denouncing the corruption of the Imperium and the way Muad'Dib's religion has been twisted. His sermons unsettle the priesthood and stir the crowds, and rumour insists he is Paul himself, returned. Who he actually is stays an open, deliberately unresolved question at this stage.

### House Corrino's move
On Salusa Secundus, the exiled Corrinos plot their way back to power. **Princess Wensicia** — daughter of the deposed Shaddam IV — is the driver. Her son **Farad'n** is the intended heir to the restoration, though he's a thoughtful, bookish, surprisingly perceptive young man, uneasy about his mother's methods and more drawn to scholarship than conquest. His Bashar and mentor is **Tyekanik**. Wensicia has had two **Laza tigers** conditioned to hunt and kill children matching the twins — keyed to particular garments — and sets the assassination in motion.

### The net closes
Alia's paranoia turns on Jessica, and the regime moves to seize her. Jessica is forced to flee Arrakeen into the deep desert, sheltering with Fremen still loyal to the Atreides (al-Fali and the Fedaykin), and she presses hard for Alia to be made to face the **trial of possession**. Duncan, meanwhile, is increasingly unable to square his bone-deep loyalty to the Atreides with what Alia has become.

The twins, through Leto's prescience, know the assassination is coming and roughly how it will unfold. Rather than hide, they choose to slip out of the sietch into the desert at night to meet it on their own terms — armed, prepared, and having even sewn the Atreides hawk crest onto the tell-tale Corrino robes.

## The threshold — where I stop

The twins move into the open desert, spot the two Laza tigers stalking them, and scramble down into a cleft in the rocks. That is exactly your page: cornered in the cleft, Leto calling to Ghanima for her blade so he can be sure of the kill.
