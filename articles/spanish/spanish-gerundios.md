# Spanish Gerundios (the *-ing* form)

The gerundio is Spanish's equivalent of the English *-ing* form (hablando = speaking). It's used with **estar** for the present continuous (*estoy hablando* — I'm speaking) and in a few other constructions.

---

## Irregular & stem-changing gerundios (the ones worth memorising)

These are the troublemakers. Most fall into clean patterns rather than being truly random.

| Infinitive | Meaning | Gerundio | What's going on |
|---|---|---|---|
| decir | to say | **diciendo** | e → i stem change |
| venir | to come | **viniendo** | e → i stem change |
| pedir | to ask for | **pidiendo** | e → i stem change |
| sentir | to feel | **sintiendo** | e → i stem change |
| repetir | to repeat | **repitiendo** | e → i stem change |
| seguir | to follow | **siguiendo** | e → i stem change |
| servir | to serve | **sirviendo** | e → i stem change |
| dormir | to sleep | **durmiendo** | o → u stem change |
| morir | to die | **muriendo** | o → u stem change |
| poder | to be able | **pudiendo** | o → u stem change |
| leer | to read | **leyendo** | i → y (vowel before ending) |
| creer | to believe | **creyendo** | i → y |
| oír | to hear | **oyendo** | i → y |
| caer | to fall | **cayendo** | i → y |
| traer | to bring | **trayendo** | i → y |
| construir | to build | **construyendo** | i → y (verbs in -uir) |
| ir | to go | **yendo** | irregular / i → y |

**The two patterns to internalise:**
1. **-ir verbs that stem-change** carry a change into the gerundio too: e→i (*pidiendo*) or o→u (*durmiendo*). If a verb stem-changes elsewhere and ends in -ir, expect it here.
2. **-er/-ir verbs whose stem ends in a vowel** turn the `i` of *-iendo* into `y`, because Spanish won't sit three vowels together: *leer → leyendo*, not *leiendo*.

---

## Regular gerundios (the easy rule)

| Verb ending | Rule | Example | Gerundio |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-ar** | drop -ar, add **-ando** | hablar | hablando |
| **-er** | drop -er, add **-iendo** | comer | comiendo |
| **-ir** | drop -ir, add **-iendo** | vivir | viviendo |

**In one line:** -ar verbs take **-ando**; -er and -ir verbs take **-iendo**. That's the whole regular rule — the only thing to remember is that -er and -ir share the same ending.

---

*Tip: the gerundio never changes for gender or number — it's invariable. Only the **estar** in front of it conjugates (estoy / estás / está + gerundio).*
