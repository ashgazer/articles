# Spanish "Would / Should / Could" (conditional & modals)

These aren't a past tense — they're the **conditional**, used for hypotheticals, politeness, and advice. The key insight: once you know the conditional endings, "should" and "could" are just the verbs **deber** and **poder** wearing them.

- **would** → the conditional tense itself (*hablaría* — I would speak)
- **should** → **deber** in the conditional (*debería* — I should)
- **could** → **poder** in the conditional (*podría* — I could)

---

## The conditional tense — regular formation ("would")

Easy rule: take the **whole infinitive** and add the endings below. Same endings for -ar, -er, and -ir verbs — no dropping anything.

| Person | Ending | hablar | Maps to English |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | -ía | hablar**ía** | I would speak |
| tú | -ías | hablar**ías** | you would speak |
| él/ella/ud. | -ía | hablar**ía** | he/she would speak |
| nosotros | -íamos | hablar**íamos** | we would speak |
| vosotros | -íais | hablar**íais** | you all would speak |
| ellos/uds. | -ían | hablar**ían** | they would speak |

**Reach for it when the English is:** "I *would* [verb]" — *I would eat more* (Comería más), *They would come* (Vendrían).

## "Should" — deber in the conditional

| Person | Form | Maps to English |
|---|---|---|
| yo | debería | I should |
| tú | deberías | you should |
| él/ella/ud. | debería | he/she should |
| nosotros | deberíamos | we should |
| vosotros | deberíais | you all should |
| ellos/uds. | deberían | they should |

**Use:** advice / mild obligation. *You should rest* (Deberías descansar), *We should go* (Deberíamos irnos).

---

## "Could" — poder in the conditional

| Person | Form | Maps to English |
|---|---|---|
| yo | podría | I could |
| tú | podrías | you could |
| él/ella/ud. | podría | he/she could |
| nosotros | podríamos | we could |
| vosotros | podríais | you all could |
| ellos/uds. | podrían | they could |

**Use:** possibility / polite requests. *Could you help me?* (¿Podrías ayudarme?), *We could go to the cinema* (Podríamos ir al cine).

## The common irregular stems (conditional)

Good news: the conditional uses the **exact same irregular stems as the future tense**, plus these same -ía endings. Learn them once, they cover both tenses.

| Infinitive | Meaning | Stem | yo form | Maps to English |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tener | to have | tendr- | tendría | I would have |
| poner | to put | pondr- | pondría | I would put |
| venir | to come | vendr- | vendría | I would come |
| salir | to leave | saldr- | saldría | I would leave |
| poder | to be able | podr- | podría | I could |
| haber | to have (aux) | habr- | habría | there would be |
| saber | to know | sabr- | sabría | I would know |
| hacer | to do/make | har- | haría | I would do/make |
| decir | to say | dir- | diría | I would say |
| querer | to want | querr- | querría | I would want/like |

*The stems always end in -r, then you add -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían.*

## Everyday polite / softening uses

The conditional is how Spanish sounds polite — worth banking these set phrases:

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me gustaría… | I would like… |
| Querría… | I would like… (more formal) |
| ¿Podrías…? | Could you…? |
| Deberías… | You should… |
| ¿Te importaría…? | Would you mind…? |
| Yo que tú, … | If I were you, … |

## Signpost: full "if…then" sentences

For proper hypotheticals — "**If** I had money, I **would** travel" — the conditional pairs with the **imperfect subjunctive** in the "if" clause:

> **Si tuviera** dinero, **viajaría.** — If I had money, I would travel.

Pattern: **Si + [imperfect subjunctive], + [conditional].**

The conditional half you already know from this sheet. The *si tuviera* half (imperfect subjunctive) is a separate topic worth its own file when you're ready — but now you know where "would" slots into it.
